About the islands

Nature, tranquility, and island charm

Camotes Islands is a group of islands located in the eastern part of Cebu. It is known for its beautiful beaches, crystal-clear waters, natural attractions, and peaceful surroundings.

It is a great destination for travelers who want to relax, enjoy nature, and experience the calm beauty of island life.
